Privacy

What happens to a prayer request after you send it.

{% endblock %} {% block content %} {% comment %} Plain promises the church can keep, nothing more. No claims of complying with any law, and every "we don't share" carries its exceptions. The retention number comes from settings, the same value purge_old_requests enforces. {% endcomment %}

What we collect

The name you enter and your request. A first name, initials, or “Anonymous” is fine. We don't ask for an email address or phone number, there are no accounts, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who reads it

Our pastors and prayer team. The people who look after this website for the church can also access stored requests, as part of keeping it running.

Services we use

Our hosting provider stores requests on the church's behalf. To keep out spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser for signs of a bot. Cloudflare receives technical details for that check, such as your IP address and browser type. The check never sends Cloudflare your name or request.

When someone may be in danger

Indiana law requires us to report suspected child abuse or neglect. If we believe someone is in danger, we may also contact the appropriate authorities.

What we never do

We never sell prayer requests or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

Requests are deleted automatically {{ retention_days }} days after they are submitted. Our web server also keeps a log of visits, with the IP address and time of each one, which is deleted after about 30 days.

This isn't an emergency service

Requests aren't read around the clock. In an emergency, call 911. For a mental health or suicide crisis, call or text 988.
